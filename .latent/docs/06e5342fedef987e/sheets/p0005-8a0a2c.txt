1. INTRODUCTION
In any organization—be it industrial, commercial, or corporate—accurate employee
attendance and work hour tracking are essential components of effective workforce
management. Most companies still rely on traditional methods such as manual registers,
biometric systems, or RFID-based attendance systems. These methods, while common, come
with several limitations such as human error, buddy punching, limited accuracy, maintenance
issues, and a lack of real-time monitoring capabilities.
With the increasing demand for automation and the rapid evolution of Internet of Things (IoT)
technologies, it has become necessary to rethink and upgrade the conventional methods of
attendance management. Our project titled “BLE-Based Employee Tracking System” aims
to bridge this technological gap by offering a smart, cost-effective, and scalable solution using
Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) beacons and ESP32 microcontrollers.
The system is designed in such a way that each employee carries a small BLE tag, specifically
using the SparkFun NanoBeacon IN100. These tags constantly emit a unique signal (UUID),
which can be detected by an ESP32-based BLE receiver placed at the main entrance of the
workplace or any designated checkpoint. The ESP32 captures the UUID, logs the exact entry
or exit time, and forwards this data to a cloud backend system (e.g., Firebase) via Wi-Fi
connectivity.
This data is then processed and displayed on a user-friendly web dashboard that can be
accessed by administrators or managers in real time. The system calculates the total active
work hours per employee on a daily basis, which helps in maintaining accurate attendance
records and streamlining payroll calculations.
Unlike traditional methods, this system provides multiple benefits including reduced human
error, improved transparency, increased data accuracy, and reduced administrative workload.
It also eliminates the need for physical touch (as required in biometric systems), which makes
it more hygienic—especially relevant in a post-pandemic world.
Furthermore, the system’s low-power design ensures that the BLE beacons can last for several
months to a year on a single coin cell battery, making it both sustainable and easy to maintain.
The ESP32-based receiver is programmable, scalable, and cost-efficient, making it suitable for
small, medium, or large enterprises.
In conclusion, the BLE-Based Employee Tracking System is an innovative step toward
automating workforce management using modern IoT solutions. It reflects the growing trend
of smart infrastructure and promises to improve overall operational efficiency, reduce costs,
and provide managers with reliable data for better decision-making.
