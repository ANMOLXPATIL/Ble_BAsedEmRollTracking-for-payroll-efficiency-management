7.2 HARDWARE REQUIREMENTS
1. ESP32 Development Board
A powerful microcontroller with built-in Wi-Fi and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)
capabilities. Acts as the BLE receiver at the workplace entrance.
2. SparkFun NanoBeacon IN100
Used as the BLE beacon that each employee will carry. Continuously emits a unique
UUID to identify the employee.
3. Coin Cell Batteries (e.g., CR2032)
Powers the BLE beacons. Chosen for their compact size and long battery life.
4. USB Cable and Power Adapter
Required to power the ESP32 board.
5. Wi-Fi Router / Access Point
Provides internet connectivity to the ESP32 for transmitting data to Firebase.
6. Computer or Laptop
Used for development, programming, and deployment of the system.
