3. ABSTRACT
The growing demand for accuracy, automation, and real-time monitoring in workforce
management has led to the need for advanced employee tracking solutions. Traditional
attendance systems such as manual registers, RFID swipes, and biometric devices often suffer
from inefficiencies, human error, lack of scalability, and insufficient data transparency. These
limitations become even more apparent in industrial or large-scale work environments where
constant monitoring and accurate data logging are critical.
To address these challenges, we have developed a BLE-Based Employee Tracking System,
a smart and efficient approach for automating employee attendance and calculating active
work hours using Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) technology. The primary goal of this project
is to eliminate manual intervention and provide a reliable solution for attendance monitoring
and work hour calculation.
The system operates by assigning each employee a BLE beacon—specifically the SparkFun
NanoBeacon IN100—which emits a unique UUID signal continuously. These signals are
detected by an ESP32-based BLE receiver placed at the entry and exit points of the
workplace. Once detected, the system logs the precise entry and exit timestamps of each
employee.
This data is then transmitted via Wi-Fi to a cloud-based backend (Firebase), where it is
processed to calculate the total number of active hours worked by each employee. The data is
then displayed on a responsive web dashboard, accessible to managers and administrators for
remote monitoring, analysis, and decision-making. The system is designed to be simple,
energy-efficient, scalable, and easy to deploy in any office or industrial setup.
By minimizing human error, eliminating the need for physical contact, and allowing real-time
data access, this solution not only streamlines attendance tracking but also significantly
improves operational efficiency. Additionally, the system is cost-effective and eco-friendly,
reducing the reliance on paper-based registers or complex biometric setups.
The BLE-Based Employee Tracking System serves as a practical example of how IoT
(Internet of Things) technologies can be effectively applied to solve real-world
administrative problems. It provides a foundation for future improvements, such as integration
with payroll systems, employee notifications, or mobile-based alert systems. This project
demonstrates the practical utility of combining hardware (ESP32, BLE beacons) with software
(Firebase, web dashboard) to deliver an end-to-end automated solution for employee
management.
